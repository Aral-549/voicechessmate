01 · Problem Statement

Chess is the world's most universal game.
But not for everyone.

285 million people with visual impairments are effectively locked out of online chess.

  • 👁️
    Chess.com & Lichess require constant visual tracking.
    Timed games — Blitz, Bullet — are physically impossible without a real-time visual board.
  • 🔊
    Screen readers read raw HTML tables cell by cell.
    6–10 second delay per move. One slow move and the clock flags. Game over.
  • 🧠
    Zero tactical context.
    No awareness of threats, forks, pins, or checks. Just raw coordinate data.
02 · Proposed Solution

Say your move. Hear the response.
Play the whole game.

You: "Knight to f3" Coach: "Knight to f3. Good development. I'll respond with pawn to c5. Your move, White."

Every interaction is natural spoken language — not commands, not codes. Just chess.

04 · Technical Approach

Solving the hard problems
nobody else bothered with.

🎙️ AssemblyAI Voice Agent (WebSocket)

Real-time PCM16 audio streamed in 25ms chunks. The agent understands chess context and calls typed tools — apply_move, describe_board, undo_move.

🔈 Dual AudioContext (Linux fix)

Speaker context at native 48kHz (prevents PulseAudio muting). Mic capture at 24kHz (what AssemblyAI requires). One of the few apps that actually works on Linux.

♟️ chess.js + full ruleset

Legal move validation, en passant, castling, promotion, threefold repetition, 50-move rule — all of it. 337 tests covering adversarial edge cases.
